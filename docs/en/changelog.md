# Changelog

## Version 1.0 (coming soon)

The first release of YAFA on Salla's Twilight React engine:

- 32 home page blocks, including the banner builder grid, products in order and tabbed products.
- Light and dark mode, with colours and a logo per mode.
- Arabic and English on every page.
- A new product page with a sticky add-to-cart bar on phones, quick buy and a WhatsApp button.
- Category pages with texts, banners and suggested products, and filters in a side drawer on phones.
- The interactive side cart and the add-to-cart notice.
- Customer account: profile, orders, wishlist, notifications, wallet.
- Custom font, a distinctive cursor, a loader and a welcome pop-up.
