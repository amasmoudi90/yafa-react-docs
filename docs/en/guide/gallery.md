# Theme gallery

Screenshots of a demo store with the YAFA theme, in English. The content (images, texts, products) is demo content: your store shows your own. The demo store's content was written in Arabic only, so it stays Arabic here; the theme's own texts (menus, buttons, labels) are in English, and the layout is left to right.
