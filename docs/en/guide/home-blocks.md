# Home page blocks

The home page is made of **blocks** that you add and order in the theme editor, then fill in. You can add the same block more than once with different content. Block names appear in English and Arabic in the editor.

## Common fields

Most blocks have these fields:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Block title / description | The heading and paragraph above the block |
| Block alignment | "Centre" (centred heading with a decorative line) or "Start" (heading on the side with a coloured line, with the "View all" link or the arrows next to it) |
| Background colour | Background colour 1 or 2 from the [store colours](./settings#store-colours-تخصيص-ألوان-المتجر) |
| Products source | One or more categories, offers, latest or chosen products |
| Items per row | For phones, tablets and computers, in animated blocks |
| Outer margin | Shows the block inside a container with margins, or full width |

## Banners and media

### Banner slider builder
A professional banner slider: per banner a computer image and a phone image, a title and description in the size and colour you choose, an animated button with a link, the content's position (top / middle / bottom, start / centre / end) and an optional side image next to the text. Ideal as the first block of the page.

::: tip Suggested sizes
2100×800 for the computer image and 800×1200 for the phone image. At these sizes the store reserves the banner's space before the image loads, so the page does not move while it loads.
:::

### Banner builder (grid)
The same banners in a grid instead of a slider: choose the number of columns per screen and the width of each banner (how many columns it takes). The whole banner is one link.

### Animated banners
Image-only banners (computer image, phone image and a link), with the number of banners per row per screen (1 to 6) and autoplay.

### Static banner(s)
One or more banners in a grid, with an optional block title, the number of columns and each banner's width.

### YAFA Two Banners
Two banners side by side, each with an image, title, description and a button with a link.

### YAFA Hero
An opening section with a title, description and button, and an optional background video (MP4/WebM or YouTube).

### YAFA Video Section
An MP4 video full width or in a container, with an optional sound button.

### YAFA Youtube
A YouTube video by its id (the part after `v=` in the link).

### YAFA Divider
Space between two blocks, with or without a line, and a vertical margin you choose (0 to 256 px).

## Quick links

Links with an image and a title to your categories or pages, in four designs:

| Block | Look |
|---|---|
| Quick links (style 1) | Cards with an image and a title, animated or in a grid |
| Quick links (style 2) | Small round / square images with the title below, up to 8 per row |
| Quick links (style 3) | Cards with a title and subtitle, with a choice of corner shape, image ratio (square or tall 2:3) and shadow |
| Quick links (mosaic) | Seven tiles of different sizes in a mosaic |

## Products

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Fixed products | A product grid from the source you choose (4 to 32 products), with a "View all" link |
| Animated products | A product slider with arrows, dots and autoplay |
| Animated products with a banner | A banner with a background, title and button beside a product slider |
| Fixed products in order | The products you pick, in exactly the order you set |
| Fixed products with tabs | Tabs, each with its own title and products source; the first tab opens first |
| Animated products with tabs | The same tabs, with an animated product slider in each |
| Single product | One featured product with its images, price and add-to-cart button |
| Brands | The logos of the brands you pick in an animated slider |

## Content and trust

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Store features (style 1 / 2 / 3) | Your store's strengths with an icon, an icon background colour, a title and a description, in three designs |
| Store numbers | Numbers that count up when they appear (customers, products…), 2 to 4 |
| Store steps | Numbered steps (such as how to buy), 2 to 4 |
| Advanced descriptive content | A title, rich text and a button with an image, and a video that plays when the image is clicked, with options to order the text and image |
| FAQ | Collapsible questions and answers (up to 15) |
| Blog | Articles from your store's blog |
| Store location (Google Maps) | Your branch's map: in Google Maps choose "Share", then "Embed a map", and copy the link from inside the code |

## Offers and contact

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Discount block (with a coupon) | A promotional text and a countdown to the offer's end date, a coupon the customer copies with one tap, and a button to the offers page, in two colours you choose |
| Contact block (WhatsApp and social) | A title and a button to your store's WhatsApp or a link you choose, with your social links |

## Salla's built-in blocks

The editor also offers Salla's own blocks, in YAFA's design:

- **Customer reviews**: your store's reviews in a slider.
- **Random customer reviews**: a changing selection of store reviews.
- **Products slider** (Salla's): a product slider with YAFA's product cards.

## Home page tips

- Start with a strong banner, then quick links to your categories, then products.
- Vary the blocks; avoid the same type twice in a row.
- Use "YAFA Divider" to adjust spacing.
- Check the page on a phone: most visitors shop from one.
