# Store pages

## Header and menus

- **Header**: logo, main menu, search, language and currency, customer account, the light / dark switch and the cart.
- The **main menu** comes from your Salla menus. Sub-menus open as a drop-down on computers and in a side menu on phones.
- **Bottom bar on phones**: home, categories, search, cart, my account.

## Product page

- A gallery with a large image and thumbnails, zoom on click.
- Name, regular and sale price, promotional title, rating, times sold and availability.
- Product options (size, colour…), quantity, the add-to-cart button, and quick buy when your store offers it.
- A sticky bar at the bottom of the screen on phones (from the settings).
- Gifting, quick order, offers, tags and instalments when your store has them.
- A WhatsApp button, then the reviews and similar products.

## Categories, search and offers

One page for every product list: categories, search results, offers, latest, tags and brands.

- A title with the number of products, and the sort menu.
- **Filters** in a side column on computers and in a side drawer on phones.
- More products load automatically as you scroll.
- The category's image, its sub-categories, and its texts, banners and suggested products from the [category page settings](./settings#category-page).

## Cart

Products with their options and quantities, notes and attached files, coupons, loyalty points, free shipping, gifting and the summary before checkout. Checkout itself is run by Salla.

With the **interactive cart** setting, the cart opens as a side drawer without leaving the page.

## Customer account

Profile, orders and each order's details (tracking, invoice, reorder, cancel, rating), wishlist, notifications and wallet.

## Blog and pages

The article list (with categories, tags and authors), the article page, information pages, customer reviews, the loyalty page and the thank-you page after an order.
